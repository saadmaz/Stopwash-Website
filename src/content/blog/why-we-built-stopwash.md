The idea behind STOPWASH did not begin with a fascination for hoses, foam or polishing cloths.

It began with a familiar feeling: arriving at a car wash and realising the next part of your day had just been handed over to a queue. The wait was slow. The result was unpredictable. The process had no rhythm.

That felt like a problem waiting for a better answer.

## The car wash was not the product. The time was.

Customers are not only paying for a clean vehicle. They are paying to get their vehicle back without losing the momentum of their day.

So STOPWASH is designed around the clock as much as the car:

- **A clear process**, so you know what will happen next.
- **A trained clean squad** that moves with purpose and notices the details.
- **A visible standard**, with the finish checked before the vehicle is handed back.

> Vehicle care should not cost you your momentum.

## Katubedda is where it starts

Our first outlet in Katubedda is where the idea meets the road. It is the first proof point for a bigger idea: vehicle care can be faster without becoming careless, more professional without becoming complicated and more consistent without losing its human touch.

## What comes next

One outlet proves a point. A network changes a category. We are building STOPWASH one stop at a time. If you want to build the next one with us, [start the franchise conversation](/franchise).

Until then, [find us in Katubedda](/locations). Keep moving.
